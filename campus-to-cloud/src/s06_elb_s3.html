Three load balancers, three jobs

ALB · layer 7

Understands HTTP: paths, hosts, headers. /api → api servers, /images → S3-backed service.

Use forWebsites, APIs, microservices, Kubernetes ingress.

NLB · layer 4

TCP/UDP. Very high performance and very low latency. Static IPs.

Use forGaming, trading, IoT, millions of connections.

GWLB

Sends traffic through third-party appliances transparently.

Use forFirewalls, intrusion detection, deep packet inspection.

From PDF 07: ALB for HTTP/HTTPS (layer 7), NLB for high-performance TCP/UDP (layer 4), Gateway Load Balancer for virtual appliances like firewalls.

Why don't big sites just buy one giant server?
Because one giant server is one giant point of failure, and it costs the same at 3 am as at peak.
Many small servers behind a load balancer survive failures, grow with demand, and shrink when everyone's asleep.
